# Deal Player Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Deal Player is an independent media player for user-provided playlists and media sources.

## Data Collection

Deal Player does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data to the developer.

Playlist information, viewing history, favorites, preferences, and application settings are stored locally on the user’s device.

## User-Provided Services

When a user connects an Xtream or M3U playlist, the app communicates directly with the service specified by the user. Deal Player does not operate, control, or store data from these services.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they are authorized to access and play any content they add to the app.

## Third-Party Services

Deal Player may retrieve movie and television metadata and artwork from TMDB. Requests to third-party services are subject to their respective privacy policies.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Deal Player does not receive or store payment card information.

## Children

Deal Player is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or privacy practices change.
