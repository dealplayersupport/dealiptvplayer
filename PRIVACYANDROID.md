# Deal Player — Android Privacy Policy
Updated: 28 October 2026
Publisher: Deal Player
Android package: com.dealplayer.android

1. About the app
Deal Player is an independent player for playlists you provide. It does not supply channels, movies, series or IPTV subscriptions. This version contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

2. Information stored on your device
Playlist names, server addresses, usernames and passwords, profiles, parental PIN settings, favorites, viewing history, progress and preferences are stored locally. Playlist credentials, trial records and cached purchase receipts are encrypted using Android Keystore. Artwork and metadata may be cached. Android application backup is disabled.

3. Services you connect
XC, M3U, stream, artwork and EPG requests go to the addresses specified by your playlist. Those services receive your IP address and the information needed for the request, including your login credentials when required. Their privacy practices are outside our control. HTTP sources do not encrypt network traffic; use HTTPS sources when available.

4. TMDB and external links
Film/series titles, years or TMDB identifiers are sent to TMDB to obtain posters, backdrops and cast information. Your playlist passwords and stream addresses are not sent to TMDB. TMDB and its image service receive your IP address and request details. Opening an external website or trailer is subject to that service's policies.
TMDB privacy policy: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy

5. Trial and purchases
The 14-day trial starts when you select Start trial. Its start time and clock checkpoints are stored locally. The permanent app unlock is a separate, one-time Google Play purchase, not a subscription. Google processes payment and may handle account, device and transaction data under its own privacy policy. Deal Player checks signed purchase receipts and stores them locally to restore access; it does not receive your payment card details. No developer-operated purchase or trial server is used in this version.
Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Retention and deletion
Local records remain until removed through the app or Android's app-storage controls. You can delete saved playlists and profiles and clear watch progress in settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data. Purchase history retained by Google and data held by your playlist provider must be managed with those services; uninstalling does not refund or delete a Google Play purchase. No email/password Deal Player account is required. Optional device sync uses an anonymous device identity.

8. Optional device sync
Starting device pairing uploads an encrypted copy of saved XC/online M3U credentials, playlist names, profile settings and movie/episode progress to our Supabase-hosted sync service. Data is decrypted only on your paired devices; the random encryption key is exchanged between devices after a security-code comparison and approval. The service processes encrypted content, anonymous device identifiers, device names, pairing public keys/codes and timestamps. Supabase also processes IP addresses and request metadata to operate and secure the service. Pairing codes expire after five minutes. Local M3U files, player/control settings, Google Play receipts and trial records are not synchronized.

Unlinking a device stops its access but cannot erase data already received by that device. Delete cloud data in Device Sync removes the shared encrypted vault and unlinks all devices; local records remain. Uninstalling alone does not delete the shared vault. Anonymous authentication records, provider logs and backups follow the service's retention practices. If all devices lose their encryption key, we cannot recover the encrypted contents.

Supabase privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

9. Intended audience
Deal Player is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. No age-verification service or identity-document collection is built into the app. Users are responsible for choosing lawful playlists appropriate for their age. Parental PIN and profile settings are controlled on the user's devices. For questions about a child's data, contact muratbeard@gmail.com.
